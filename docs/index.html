About me

I discovered statistics through a high-school project on local air quality, and I have been hooked on the question “how sure are we?” ever since.

At ENSAI I focus on statistical modelling and machine learning, with a soft spot for time series and survey data. I like projects where the hard part is not the algorithm but understanding the data and explaining the result to non-specialists.

Outside the classroom:
