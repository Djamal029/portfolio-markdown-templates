Survey Sampling Simulation

Comparing simple random, stratified and cluster sampling designs by simulation on a synthetic population.

  • R
  • Stratified sampling
  • Monte Carlo

Simulated 10,000 samples per design to compare bias and variance of the estimators. Stratified sampling divided the variance by three for the same sample size.
